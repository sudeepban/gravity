🌌 Welcome to Gravity Lab!

An interactive playground for exploring gravity, motion, forces — and the beautiful calculus that connects them.

📈 Position → Velocity → Acceleration

position y(t)  —d/dt→  velocity v(t)  —d/dt→  acceleration a(t)

Velocity is the derivative (slope) of position. Acceleration is the derivative of velocity. Going backwards, the integral (area under the curve) of acceleration gives the change in velocity, and the area under velocity gives the change in position. Hover over the live graphs at the bottom — you'll see the tangent-line slope on one curve exactly match the value of the next, and the shaded area match the change in the previous one. That's the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, live!

🪂 Drop Lab

  • Drag the object up and down — flick it to throw it! Release to let physics take over.
  • Toggle air resistance and switch objects: with air OFF, a feather and a hammer fall identically (Galileo was right — astronaut David Scott proved it on the Moon in 1971!).
  • Try Race vs feather mode and different planets.

🎯 Cannon

  • Click & drag on the sky to aim — drag farther for more power, release to FIRE!
  • Hit the target to score. Watch the velocity split into vx (constant!) and vy (changing — gravity only pulls down).
  • A 45° launch gives maximum range on flat ground. Can you prove it?

🪐 Orbit Sandbox

  • Click & drag anywhere to launch a planet — the drag sets its velocity, and a dashed line predicts its path.
  • Newton's insight: an orbit is just falling forever and missing the ground. Too slow → spiral in. Too fast → fly away. Just right → ellipse!
  • Try the presets, then make your own solar system. Planets can merge!

🚀 Force Lab — F = ma

  • Hold the THRUST buttons (or ←/→ keys) to push the rocket sled.
  • Same force, more mass → less acceleration. That's Newton's 2nd law: a = F/m.
  • Watch the Newton's-laws box: it highlights whichever law is in action right now.
