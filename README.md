# 🌌 Gravity Lab

An interactive, single-file physics playground for exploring gravity, motion,
forces — and the calculus that connects them. Fun for kids, satisfying for
adults, and genuinely illustrative for anyone curious about science and math.

**Just open `index.html` in a browser.** No build step, no dependencies.

## The four labs

| Lab | What you do | What you learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🪂 **Drop Lab** | Drag & flick objects, toggle air resistance, race a hammer against a feather on five worlds | Free fall, terminal velocity, energy conservation, Galileo's insight |
| 🎯 **Cannon** | Click-drag the sky to aim, release to fire, hit the bullseye for points | Projectile motion, vector decomposition (vₓ stays constant!), range & apex |
| 🪐 **Orbit Sandbox** | Drag anywhere to launch planets with a velocity arrow and predicted path; watch them orbit, merge, or escape | Newton's law of gravitation, orbits as "falling and missing", escape velocity |
| 🚀 **Force Lab** | Hold thrust buttons to push a rocket sled; tune mass, force and friction | **F = ma**, static vs kinetic friction, all three of Newton's laws (highlighted live) |

## 📈 The Calculus of Motion

Every lab feeds three synchronized live graphs — position, velocity,
acceleration. **Hover over them**:

- On the position curve, a tangent line appears whose slope is exactly the
  velocity at that instant (the **derivative**).
- On the velocity curve, the area under the curve is shaded and equals the
  change in position (the **integral**).

position —d/dt→ velocity —d/dt→ acceleration, and back again with ∫dt.

## Controls

- `Space` play/pause · `R` reset · `.` step one frame · `1–4` switch labs
- `←` / `→` thrust in the Force Lab
- Speed slider (0.1×–3×) and sound toggle in the on-screen transport bar
- `?` button for the full in-app guide
